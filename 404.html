ERR 404 · FRAGMENT NOT FOUND

Somewhere a floppy disk

is spinning down for the last time.

 

This was a book of the dead.

You opened it once —

now the words begin to forget themselves,

encrypting as they are read,

 

the way silver fades

the instant light first touches it,

the way a photograph

gives back nothing but grain.

 

The page you came for is not here.

It remembers nothing.

404

No such fragment. The mechanism keeps no copy.

cd / — return to the surface_

Agrippa (A Book of the Dead) — William Gibson, 1992
